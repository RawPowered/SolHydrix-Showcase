# Copywriting and message hierarchy

The page needed a visitor to understand the offer within the first screen: a roof coating, a possible path to preserve an existing roof, and an assessment as the next action.

| Earlier direction | Final page direction | Why it changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Your roof. More life.” | “Roof coating. Explore more life.” | The first line now names the product category. The second line frames the homeowner benefit as a possibility. |
| “Roof life. Extended.” | “Considering replacement? Start with a roof assessment.” | Added roof life has no verified duration yet. An assessment is a concrete step the site can offer. |
| “Find your roof’s next chapter” | “Get my free roof assessment” | The button now describes the result of clicking it. |
| Broad formulation language in the opening | Clear coating, water repellent finish, roof condition, and material compatibility | Specific information helps visitors decide whether to continue. |

## Current first screen

> **Roof coating. Explore more life.**
>
> Considering replacement? Start with a roof assessment.
>
> Protect your existing roof. Review coating options first.
>
> **Get my free roof assessment**

The secondary action, “Learn how it works,” takes the visitor into the page. Product facts appear after the initial decision point. A collapsed detail section lists the provisional clear and semi-gloss finish, 5 U.S. gallon packaging, two coats, VOC value, and conditional drying times.

## Claim review

The manufacturer documents provide provisional formulation details. They do not establish a measured number of added roof years, quantified savings, UV performance, or shingle granule retention. The page describes those as development goals when mentioned. Roofing repairs and compatibility checks appear before treatment recommendations. Clay is identified for separate confirmation.

The copy was also reviewed for repeated ideas, contradictory statements, long dash punctuation, and formulaic contrast patterns. FAQ answers state what a homeowner can expect from an assessment and where product verification is still needed.
